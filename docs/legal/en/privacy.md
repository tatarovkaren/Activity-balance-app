# Privacy Policy

Effective {{effectiveDate}}. Version {{version}}.

This Policy explains what personal data the Activity Balance service (the "Service") collects, why, where it is stored and how you can control it. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

## 1. Who is responsible

Data controller (operator): {{operator}}, {{operatorId}}, address: {{address}}. Contact for data questions: {{email}}.

## 2. What we process

- **Account data:** email address, name, Google, Apple, Yandex ID or VK ID account identifier if you sign in with them.
- **Profile:** sex, date of birth, height, goal weight, step goal, interface language and theme.
- **Health data** (special category): weight, body measurements (waist, neck, hips, etc.), body fat percentage, nutrition (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates), steps, workouts, heart rate, "unusual day" marks with a reason (illness, cycle, feast, etc.).
- **Food photos**, if you use photo portion estimates: pictures of a meal next to your hand and your hand size for scale.
- **Data from files and trackers** that you import yourself (for example, a FatSecret export, Health Connect, Apple Health).
- **Coach connection:** invite code, coach comments, reports you send to your coach.
- **Technical data:** device and browser type, system language, sign-in times, error logs.
- **Marketing consent** and the address for newsletters, if you gave it.

We do not collect your location and do not access your contacts.

## 3. Why and on what legal basis

- **Running the Service** (energy balance calculations, trends, forecasts, sync): account data, profile, health data, imports. Basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a); 152-FZ Art. 9 and 10(2)(1)) and performance of our agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Photo portion estimates:** food photos, hand size. Basis: separate consent before your first photo.
- **Coach access:** the data listed on the connection screen. Basis: your consent, given when you enter a coach's code.
- **News and offers:** email, name. Basis: separate consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); 38-FZ "On Advertising" Art. 18).
- **Security and bug fixing:** technical data. Basis: performance of our agreement and legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Legal obligations:** data as required. Basis: law (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); 152-FZ Art. 6(1)(2)).

We do not sell data, do not show ads based on your health data, and do not make decisions with legal effects on you based on it. The Service's calculations are automated estimates for your information.

## 4. Where data is stored

4.1. Without signing in, data stays only on your device (browser or app storage) and is not sent to us.

4.2. After you sign in, data is stored in the cloud. Data of Russian citizens is recorded, stored and updated in databases located in Russia (152-FZ Art. 18(5)). Data of EU users is stored on servers in the EU.

4.3. We use service providers (processors) that handle data on our behalf: hosting, cloud database, email delivery, photo estimation. They are bound by confidentiality and security obligations. Current list: {{processors}}.

4.4. International transfers (for example, sending food photos to a photo estimation service abroad) take place only as permitted by GDPR Chapter V (adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses) and 152-FZ Art. 12, and with your consent where required.

## 5. Who we share data with

- **Your coach**, only if you connected one yourself, and only the data listed on the connection screen. You can disconnect a coach in your profile.
- **Processors** acting on our behalf (section 4.3).
- **Authorities**, only when required by law.

## 6. How long we keep data

- Account data, profile and health data: while you have an account, deleted within 30 days after you delete your account or withdraw consent, unless the law requires longer storage.
- Food photos: deleted after you confirm the entry, unless you saved the photo as a reference.
- Technical logs: up to 90 days.
- Marketing consent: until you withdraw it.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- find out what data we process and get a copy (your profile has a backup export);
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data and account;
- withdraw any consent at any time; this does not affect processing before withdrawal, but without consent to health data processing the Service cannot make calculations;
- object to or restrict processing;
- receive your data in a machine-readable format (portability);
- lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your EU country or with Roskomnadzor (rkn.gov.ru) in Russia.

Send requests to {{email}}. We will reply within one month (GDPR Art. 12), and for users in Russia within 10 business days (152-FZ Art. 14 and 20).

## 8. How we protect data

- data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- database-level access control: each user sees only their own data, a coach sees only the permitted fields of their clients;
- processors only get access needed for their task;
- passwordless sign-in (Google, Apple, Yandex ID, VK ID or an email code), so we do not store passwords.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses browser storage (localStorage) and strictly necessary cookies to remember your sign-in, settings and on-device data. There are currently no analytics or advertising services. If we add any, we will ask for your consent first.

## 10. Children

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you learn that a child has given us data, write to {{email}} and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We update this Policy when the Service or the law changes. The new version is published in the Service with its date. We will announce material changes in advance and ask for new consent where required.
