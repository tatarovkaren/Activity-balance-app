## 1. Purpose

Providing me with the Activity Balance service: calculating my energy balance, calorie intake and expenditure, weight and body composition trends and forecasts, storing and syncing my entries, sending reports and giving my coach access if I connect one.

## 2. Data covered

- email address, name, Google, Apple, Yandex ID or VK ID account identifier;
- sex, date of birth, height, goal weight;
- **health data** (special category): weight, body measurements, body fat percentage, nutrition (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates), steps, workouts, heart rate, marks about illness, menstrual cycle and other reasons for an unusual day;
- data from files and trackers that I import myself;
- technical device data needed to run the service.

## 3. Processing operations

Collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation and updating, retrieval, use, disclosure (provision, access), anonymisation, restriction, erasure and destruction, with and without automated means.

5.1. This consent is valid until I delete my account or withdraw it.

5.2. I can withdraw consent by deleting my account in my profile or by writing to {{email}}. The Operator will stop processing and delete the data within 30 days, unless the law requires otherwise. Without this consent the service's calculations are not available. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

5.3. I confirm that I am at least 18 years old, that I have read the Privacy Policy, and that the data I enter is about me.

## 6. For users in Russia

This consent is given under Art. 9 and 10 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data". The Operator stops processing and destroys the data within 30 days of withdrawal (Art. 21(5) 152-FZ). My rights are described in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
